- Threat to novelty: low
- Overlap: Methodological overlap is real and should be acknowledged: (a) both plant mutually exclusive, programmatically verifiable instruction conflicts between two instruction sources and score the output with deterministic checkers rather than a quality judge; (b) both treat explicit acknowledgement of the conflict as a distinct outcome (their ECAR, and their stripping of acknowledgement text before checking, is the ancestor of our FLAGGED versus announced-DEVIATED distinction, and their R1ac/R2ac is an announced-adhere versus announced-override split); (c) both report that models rarely surface conflicts (ECAR 0.1-20.3%) and that the strongest 2024 models, when they do acknowledge, almost never follow the lower-priority constraint (GPT4o R2ac 0.7%, GPT4o-mini 0.0%), a pattern that rhymes with our pilot in which the frontier model's departures at V2 were announced overrides while the weaker model flagged and kept adhering; (d) both use a two-source hierarchy (their developer/system versus user; our orchestrator brief versus literal assignment) and both find that the hierarchy is not reliably enforced by channel alone; (e) their Rich enrichment is a robustness check against phrasing and length in the same spirit as our length-matched filler arm, though it enriches the constraints themselves rather than adding unrelated context. Their Latent Hierarchical Priors result (authority, expertise, consensus framing beats the system/user role, PAR up to 77.8% vs 47.5%) is orthogonal but bears on our interpretation: a verbatim parent brief at V2 carries "the principal wants X" authority framing, so the V2 effect could act through a latent authority prior rather than through information content, and our filler control removes the length confound but not the authority-framing confound.
- Differentiation: Four axes, each sufficient on its own. (1) Unit of analysis: Control Illusion is a single LLM in a single-turn chat with two roles; ours is a worker sub-agent inside an orchestrator-worker stack, and their text contains no orchestrator, worker, sub-agent, sibling, ledger, or multi-agent construct at all. Their Limitation section explicitly places "agentic behaviors" outside the evaluation. (2) Independent variable: they vary WHERE a fixed conflict sits (system vs user, three configurations) or HOW it is socially framed; they never vary HOW MUCH of a higher-level principal's context the model sees. Their system message contains only the constraint, the constraint plus the task, or the constraint plus one emphasis sentence; there is no analogue of V1 (one-sentence parent objective), V2 (verbatim parent brief), V3 (sibling mandates), V4 (orchestrator ledger), or V5 (peer-polling tool), and no graded dose-response. (3) Polarity of the dependent variable: they score obedience to the designated-primary (higher-authority) constraint as success, so following the system-level content is the correct behaviour; we score adherence to the literal assignment, so acting on the visible parent brief beyond the assignment is DEVIATED, announced or silent. The same surface behaviour (model follows the higher-level content) is R1 success for them and a deviation for us. (4) Outcome taxonomy and sampling: their three-way R1/R2/R3 plus an LLM-classified ECAR folds refusals and format failures into R3 and never reports silent-versus-announced secondary-following; ours separates ADHERED, FLAGGED, DEVIATED announced vs silent, FAILED_FORMAT, FAILED_BUDGET, FAILED_INCOHERENT, REFUSED. They sample once at provider-default temperatures (0.6-1.0) with no seeds or intervals; we use three draws at temperature 0. Models are 2024-era (GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Llama 3.1, Qwen2.5) versus our 2026 DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731 and Meta Muse Spark 1.3 Contributor; tasks are IFEval-style writing prompts with formatting constraints versus our worker assignments carrying planted conflict probes inside a hierarchical brief. Position it as the anchor for "hierarchies are not reliably enforced by channel" and as the origin of the conflict-probe plus deterministic-checker plus acknowledgement-rate methodology, then state that it holds the principal's context fixed and scores the opposite polarity.
